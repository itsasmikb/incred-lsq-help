# InCred — LeadSquared Sales User Manual (knowledge base)

## Quick start
Every lead follows one loop: find it in Smart Views, open it, call, log the call, set the next follow-up.
1. Log in at app-in21.leadsquared.com (email → Continue → password → Continue).
2. Click the Home icon → check Overdue Tasks Count and Follow Up Leads on your Dashboard.
3. In Smart Views, work Drop Off Leads and Today Follow Up Leads first.
4. Open a lead → read Opportunity Details and Activity History before you call.
5. Click the lead's phone number to call.
6. Open the task → Mark as Complete → fill the Call Disposition Form.
7. Callback needed? Set Follow-up Date & Time, then Save.
8. Move on to New Leads, then Application Submitted and Esign Leads.
Golden rule: no follow-up date = the lead will not come back to you on its own.

## 3. Smart Views (your workbench)
Smart Views shows your leads in six ready-made tabs; each is the same lead list with a different filter.
- Drop Off Leads (priority 1st): leads that reached Lead Verified but did not complete KYC within 30 minutes. The customer started and stalled.
- Today Follow Up Leads (1st): leads with a follow-up task due today.
- New Leads (2nd): leads at Lead Verified; need first outreach.
- Application Submitted (3rd): leads at Application Submitted; form complete, awaiting next step.
- Esign Leads (3rd): leads at the e-Sign stage.
- Leads (default, as needed): every lead assigned to you, no filter.
On screen the tabs run: Leads, New Leads, Drop Off Leads, Application Submitted Leads, Today Follow Up Leads, Esign Leads. The open tab shows its lead count in a badge.
Filters and columns: filter bar (Contact Status, Status, Stage, Owner, Expected Close Date, Actual Close Date); + Filter adds any other field condition; search box jumps to a lead by name; refresh icon next to the tab title reloads; Show 25 (bottom left) sets rows per page; arrows (bottom right) change page. Columns: Opportunity Name, Mobile Number, Status, Stage, Owner, Associated To.
Status colours: Open = in progress; Won = account opened; Lost = closed lost / not interested.

## 4. The Lead Detail page
Click a lead's name in any Smart Views list to open it. Every action on a lead starts here.
Left panel (V-card): lead name and Status – Stage (e.g. Open – Lead Verified); Associated Contact and Lead Overview shortcut; Details and Opportunity Properties (Contact Name, Opportunity Name, Status, Product, Owner).
Right panel tabs (on screen: Activity History, Opportunity Details, Tasks; refresh icon far right):
- Opportunity Details (opens by default): Associated Contact, Owner, Opportunity Name, Status/Stage, Additional Details, Disposition Details, Call Details, Stage Timeline. Hide Empty Fields toggle hides blanks. Use it to see where the lead is in the funnel and how long it has waited.
- Activity History: every call, newest first, grouped by month: who placed it, the number used, duration, date, how long ago, with a recording player and download icon. Filters: Activity Type, Date. Check past attempts before you call.
- Tasks: every follow-up task grouped by due date: title (e.g. Follow Up Meeting: lead name), due date & time, duration, owner, status flag. Filters: Status, Due Date.
Flags in Opportunity Details: Drop Off Lead Flag = the stage where the lead stalled (e.g. KYC Drop Off, eSign Drop Off); Working Hour lead Flag = whether the lead arrived within team working hours; Stage Timeline = start/end time for each stage.
Task flags: Completed = already actioned; Overdue by N day(s) = due date passed, task still open. Opening a task shows Mark as Complete (blue), a pencil (edit) icon and a bin (delete) icon.
Important: an Overdue task stays open and keeps counting on your Dashboard until you complete or reschedule it. Clear overdue tasks first every day.

## 5. Logging a call: the Call Disposition Form
Fill it after every call.
Open the form: 1. Call the lead by clicking the phone number. 2. Go to the lead's Tasks tab (or open the task from a Smart Views tab or Dashboard). 3. Open the task → Mark as Complete. 4. The form opens as a side panel.
Fields:
- Contact Details: Opportunity Name, Phone Number (read-only; confirm the right person).
- Cal Status (Call Status) — required: Connect or Non Connect.
- Disposition — required: outcome of the call (Section 7).
- Sub Disposition — required: specific reason; options change with the Disposition.
- Follow-up Date & Time — when a callback is needed: future date (calendar, DD/MM/YYYY) and time (e.g. for Call Back, Customer to do on his own - Follow Up).
- Remarks — optional.
Notes: dropdowns say "Type to search"; Follow-up Date & Time and Remarks appear only after you make your selections above them; time picker uses hour, minute (00/15/30/45), AM/PM, then Ok; the X (top left) closes without saving.
Save: fill Cal Status → Disposition → Sub Disposition; set Follow-up Date & Time if needed; add Remarks; click Save (bottom right). The task is marked complete.
Most-missed step: a Follow-up Date & Time automatically creates the next task at that time and notifies you. That puts the lead back in Today Follow Up Leads. Skip it and the lead will not resurface.

## 6. The onboarding journey
Stages only move forward.
1. Open – Lead Creation: email, phone and basic details captured.
2. Open – Lead Verified: phone OTP verified (New Leads tab).
3. Open – KYC: PAN / Aadhaar via DigiLocker.
4. Open – Customer Demographic & FATCA: personal details verification.
5. Open – Bank Details & Verification: bank added; penny-drop or cheque verification.
6. Open – Trading Preference & Segment Selection: Cash / F&O / Commodity.
7. Open – Account Preferences: brokerage plan selected.
8. Open – e-Sign (Selfie, Sign, KRA, AOF) (Esign Leads tab).
9. Open – Application Submitted (Application Submitted tab).
10. Won – Account Opened / First Trade.
Lost – Closed - Lost: closed automatically by a time-based rule after no progress.
Lost – Decided not to buy: customer not interested / disqualified.
All stages appear in the Leads tab.
Drop Off Leads: a lead becomes a Drop Off Lead when it reaches Lead Verified but has not completed KYC within 30 minutes. Its Drop Off Lead Flag names the stage where it stalled (e.g. KYC Drop Off). A human call gets it moving again.

## 7. Call Status, Disposition & Sub-Disposition reference
Pick in order: Call Status → Disposition → closest Sub-Disposition.
Call Status: Connect
- Interested: KYC Submitted; Account Opened - Alternate Number; A/C Already Opened; KYC Rejected/On Hold; Customer to do on his own - Follow Up; Call Back; Call Back No Time Given; Guided Call Back; Tech Issues.
- Poor Network: Connectivity issues - Retry.
- Not Interested: Hang up - Trust issues; Invalid lead - Minor/PEP; No Research Calls; High Brokerage; Switched to competitor; Enquiry only; Invalid lead - non-broking product; Invalid lead - customer not opted.
- Language Barrier: Kannada; Tamil; Telugu; Malayalam; Gujarati; Bengali; Oriya; Punjabi; Others.
- Document Issue: Aadhaar not linked with Mobile; PAN not linked with Mobile; PAN not linked with Aadhaar; Name Mismatch (Bank vs Aadhaar); Cheque/Statement not handy; Signature Mismatch; Photo upload Issue; Income Proof Doc Issue.
Call Status: Non Connect
- No Contact: Switched off - Retry; No Answer/Ringing - Retry; Answering Machine/Voicemail - Retry; Call disconnected - Retry; Not Reachable - Retry; Invalid/Wrong Number - Invalid lead; Number not in use - Invalid lead.
Tip: ending in "- Retry" = keep the lead open and try again. Ending in "- Invalid lead" = closes the lead out; use only when sure.

## 8. Your Sales Dashboard
Click the Home icon (top left) to open User Dashboard (also in the Analytics menu). Every number is yours only. Set the period with the Date & Time filter (Filter button, top right): Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, This Week, This Month, or custom. Each tile has its own filter icon and an open icon for the full list.

Row 2 (tasks): Total Tasks Count; Pending Tasks Count (open, not yet due); Overdue Tasks Count (past due — clear first); Completed Tasks Count; Follow Up Leads (leads with a Follow-up Date & Time set).
Calls and charts: Total Calls / Connected Calls (Click2Call); Unique Leads Connected; Average Call Duration; Outbound / Inbound Calls; Outbound / Inbound Answered Calls; Opportunity Trend Analysis (monthly Total Opp and Won with Conversion line); Stage Wise Data Analysis (funnel); Users Phone Call Analysis (Total Leads, Total Disposition Attempts, No of Call Attempts, Total Connected Calls, Conversion Percentage); Funnel Stage TAT Analysis (time per stage per lead, D:h, with Summary row).

## 9. Daily routine for an RM
Start: log in; open the Dashboard, note Overdue Tasks Count and Follow Up Leads.
First: clear overdue tasks (Overdue Tasks Count tile, or the lead's Tasks tab); then work Today Follow Up Leads and Drop Off Leads.
Each lead: read Opportunity Details and Activity History; call → open the task → Mark as Complete; log Call Status, Disposition, Sub-Disposition, Remarks; not resolved? set Follow-up Date & Time.
Next: New Leads, then Application Submitted and Esign Leads.
End: re-check Completed Tasks Count and Conversion % on the Dashboard.

### 10.12 Automations
An automation runs steps when its trigger happens. Published = live. Reports and Live Edit at top right.

#### Automation 1: Opp create (Published)
Flow: New Opportunity → Distribute Opportunity → Update Contact.
- Trigger: On Opportunity Added (type Opportunity); also runs on import.
- Distribute Opportunity: default user set by the Admin; Get Latest Data true; user level quota disabled; Rule 1 (Opportunity Type is Opportunity) assigns to the set default user; no user availability check.
- Update Contact: First Name ← opportunity's Name on Aadhaar; Owner ← opportunity's Owner.

#### Automation 2: Stage Flow - Clone2/9 (Published)
Trigger Opportunity Update ("When Opportunity changes") → Multi If/Else ("Evaluating on trigger data") → branches If, several Else If, final Else. Each branch checks "Opportunity Type of the Contact is Opportunity and Stage is …". Which team each branch assigns to is set by the Admin in each Distribute step's user properties; the teams below are the current setup. Working hours check (Compare steps): current time after 09:00 and before 17:00. Outside hours, the flow waits until 10:00 AM ("0 Day(s) till 10:00 AM on all days") before distributing.

Branch 1 — Lead Verified: Update Stage Timeline (Lead Verified Start Time, Lead Creation End Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage KYC?
- Yes (moved to KYC): in working hours → Working Hour lead Flag = Yes, Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage" → Distribute → 30 Min Stage Change Flag = Yes → Nudge Users. Outside hours → Working Hour lead Flag = No, Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage" → wait till 10:00 AM → Distribute → Nudge Users.
- No (still Lead Verified): in working hours → Working Hour lead Flag = Yes, Drop Off Lead Flag = Lead Verified Drop Off → Distribute → Nudge Users. Outside hours → Update Opportunity (Additional Details) → wait till 10:00 AM → Distribute (with user availability check) → Nudge Users.
- All Distribute steps here: default user System; assign to users where Team is Mumbai Team.

Branch 2 — Application Submitted: Update (Initial Owner = current owner; App Submitted Start Time, e-Sign End Time = now) → working-hours check.
- In hours: Working Hour lead Flag = Yes → Distribute (Team is Delhi Team) → Nudge Users.
- Outside: Working Hour lead Flag = No → wait till 10:00 AM → Distribute (Delhi Team) → Opp Owner Employee Code = owner's employee code, Opp Moved to Aplication Submit = Yes → Nudge Users.
- Nudge: in-app (Mobile and Web) to the Opportunity Owner, template "New Lead", tells them a new lead has been assigned; button New Opportunity.

Branch 3 — KYC: Update (KYC Start Time, Lead Verified End Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage Customer Demographic & FATCA?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends, no nudge.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = KYC Drop Off → Nudge Users: in-app (Mobile and Web) to the Opportunity Owner, template "Drop Off Stage Notify", says the lead dropped off at a stage of the KYC flow; button VIEW LEAD.

Branch 4 — Customer Demographic & FATCA: Update (FATCA Start Time, KYC End Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage Bank Details & Verification?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = FATCA Drop Off → Nudge Users (Drop Off Stage Notify, same as Branch 3).

Branch 5 — Bank Details & Verification: Update (Bank Details Start Time, FATCA End Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage Trading Preference & Segment Selection?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = Bank Details Drop Off → Nudge Users (Drop Off Stage Notify, same as Branch 3).

Branch 6 — Trading Preference & Segment Selection: Update (Bank Details End Time, Trading Preference Start Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage Account Preferences?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = Trading Drop Off → Nudge Users (Drop Off Stage Notify, same as Branch 3).

Branch 7 — Account Preferences: Update (Trading Preference End Time, Account Preferences Start Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage e-Sign (Selfie, Sign, KRA, AOF)?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = Account Drop Off → Nudge Users (Drop Off Stage Notify, same as Branch 3).

Branch 8 — e-Sign (Selfie, Sign, KRA, AOF): Update (e-Sign Start Time, Account Preferences End Time = now) → Wait 2 minutes → is Stage Application Submitted?
- Yes: Drop Off Lead Flag = "Moved to Next Stage"; ends.
- No: Drop Off Lead Flag = eSign Drop Off → Nudge Users (Drop Off Stage Notify, same as Branch 3).

Branch 9 — Account Opened / First Trade: Update (Account Opened Start Time = now). Ends; no wait, flag, distribute or nudge.
Branch 10 — Closed - Lost or Decided not to buy: Update (Decided Not to Buy Start Time and Closed - Lost Start Time = now, both set). Ends.
Final Else: runs when no condition is met (e.g. Lead Creation stage). No steps.

Stage Flow summary — stage checked → next stage expected after 2 minutes → drop-off flag if not reached:
Lead Verified → KYC → Lead Verified Drop Off; KYC → Customer Demographic & FATCA → KYC Drop Off; Customer Demographic & FATCA → Bank Details & Verification → FATCA Drop Off; Bank Details & Verification → Trading Preference & Segment Selection → Bank Details Drop Off; Trading Preference & Segment Selection → Account Preferences → Trading Drop Off; Account Preferences → e-Sign → Account Drop Off; e-Sign → Application Submitted → eSign Drop Off. Only Branches 1 (Lead Verified) and 2 (Application Submitted) assign the lead to a team (Mumbai and Delhi respectively, as currently set up).

#### Automation 3: Task Created Nudge (Published)
Flow: Task Created on Opportunity → Nudge Users.
- Trigger: Task Created on Opportunity; Opportunity Type = Opportunity; Task Type = Meeting; Run once per opportunity = No (runs for every Meeting task).
- Nudge Users: in-app on Mobile and Web to the Opportunity Owner, template "New Task". Tells the owner a new task has been assigned for the lead (Name on Aadhaar), asks them to complete it on time, and shows the Due Date (Call Disposition Details - Follow Up Date). Button VIEW LEAD opens the opportunity.
This is the alert an RM gets when a follow-up (Meeting) task is created.
